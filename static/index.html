❓ How to play 

    
Poker in 60 seconds

    
    
      
  • You get 2 private cards. Up to 5 shared cards come out in the middle (flop 3, turn 1, river 1).
    • 
      
  • Your best 5-card hand from all 7 wins the pot.
    • 
      
  • Hand ranks, best first: Royal flush › Straight flush › Four of a kind › Full house › Flush › Straight › Three of a kind › Two pair › Pair › High card.
    • 
      
  • Fold = give up this hand. Check = pass for free. Call = match the bet. Raise = bet more so others must pay to stay in.
    • 
      
  • Pot odds = what you must pay ÷ the pot after you pay. If your win chance is higher, calling makes money over time.
    • 
    

    
How this app works

    
    
      
  • Drex bots: every bot move is a Drex call. Drex scores each legal move, and each personality uses those odds its own way.
    • 
      
  • 💡 Coach bars: on your turn, Drex's odds for your moves (gold = its pick). 🎓 Teach me reviews your moves after the hand.
    • 
      
  • 🧠 Brain-peek and ⏪ Replay: after a hand, see every bot's cards, its odds and why it moved.
    • 
      
  • Chips carry over between games. Only a player at 0 starts again at 1000. 🌐 Play online to share a room code with friends.
    • 
    

    
Shortcuts: F fold · C check/call · H raise ½ pot · P raise pot · A all-in · N next hand · B brain-peek
